How to Use the Tatkal Ticket Booking Extension

Fill in Your Login, Train and Payment Details

  1. Fill in your login details. Don't worry, your information is safe and stored securely in your Chrome local storage.
  2. Enter the train you want to book: train number, From and To station codes, journey date, quota and class. You can use the Go to IRCTC Website button to find the details.
    • Use the train's own station codes. For example, train 22178 runs to CSMT, so search BSB to CSMT, not MMCT. Otherwise IRCTC stops to ask you to confirm a different station.
  3. Booking General tickets on the first day they open (60 days ahead)? Turn on Opening Day Booking.
  4. By default the extension pays through BHIM/UPI with Paytm UPI, which is quicker than other payment methods. You can change this in Payment Details.
  5. If you want the extension to click Pay & Book for you and show the QR code page, check the box labeled Pay & Book (Show QR Code Page). Leave it off if you prefer to click Pay yourself.
  6. Timer Details:
    1. Schedule Date: the day the extension wakes up. For Tatkal, that's the day before your journey.
    2. Booking Opens At: when IRCTC opens booking. It is set for you when you pick the class:
      • 10:00:00 for AC classes.
      • 11:00:00 for Sleeper and other non-AC classes.
      • 08:00:00 for Opening Day Booking.
    3. Search Seconds Before: default is 30 seconds. The extension searches trains this much earlier, while IRCTC is still calm, and waits on the train list. The first seat check goes out right after the opening time.
    4. Refresh Time (ms): default is 5000 milliseconds (5 seconds). The extension checks seats again every 2 seconds for the first 15 seconds after opening, then every Refresh Time.
    5. Login Minutes Before: default is 2 minutes. The extension logs in this many minutes before booking opens.
  7. Once you've filled all the details, click on the Save Settings button.

The timer follows your computer's clock (IST), not the clock shown on the IRCTC page, which runs a few seconds fast. Keep your computer's Set time automatically option on. We recommend using the default timer settings.

Auto Booking Switch

  1. If the switch is turned on, the extension will run automatically whenever you visit the IRCTC website and attempt to book tickets using the provided settings and passenger details.
  2. If the switch is turned off, the extension will not run on the IRCTC page.
  3. If the switch is on and the Schedule Date is set, the extension will open IRCTC and log in at the specified minutes (2 minutes by default) before booking opens, and the Go to IRCTC Website button will disappear.
  4. If the switch is on and there are less than the specified minutes left before booking opens, the Book Ticket on IRCTC button will appear, allowing you to start manually.

How to Add Passengers for Tatkal Booking 🚆

Adding a New Passenger
  • Select the Passenger List tab.
  • Click on Add Passenger and fill in the Name, Age, Gender, Seat Preference and Food Choice.
  • Names can use only letters and spaces, 3 to 16 characters. Write initials without dots, for example R Kumar, not R. Kumar.
  • Click the Save Icon button to save them.
  • Now select the checkbox and the added passenger will be selected for booking.
  • If you don’t want to book for a passenger, simply uncheck or delete them.
Food Choice
  • On trains with meals, the extension fills in each passenger's Food Choice.
  • Some trains, like Tejas, show one I don't want Food/Beverages option for the whole booking. If every selected passenger is set to No Food, the extension ticks it and confirms IRCTC's pop-up, so you aren't charged for meals.
Infants Without Berth
  • Turn on Have Infant Without Berth? and add the infant's name, age and gender.
Using Master Data Passengers
  • Select the Passenger Master Data tab to use names saved in your IRCTC account.
  • Click on Add Passenger and enter the passenger's first name or the exact name as listed in IRCTC's master data.
  • You can add multiple passengers by following the above step.
  • Example: If your IRCTC master data contains Ajay Singh and Rahul Singh, add Ajay and Rahul as separate entries.

Note: Master Data passengers are auto-filled during booking.

⚠ Important: You can either use Master Data OR New Passenger Data—not both at the same time.

Before Booking Starts ⏰

  • Your IRCTC account must be Aadhaar verified to book Tatkal tickets.
  • Keep your Aadhaar-linked mobile nearby. Some trains ask for an OTP sent to it.
  • Keep your UPI app open and unlocked, so you can approve the payment quickly.
  • Keep only one IRCTC tab open, and don't log in on the IRCTC app or another browser at the same time.

What to Do on the IRCTC Site While Booking? 🎟️

  • Step 1: Login. The extension fills in your user ID and password and signs in. If IRCTC shows a captcha, a prompt will appear: enter the captcha and press Enter. You can also turn on Auto Solve to fill it for you.
  • Step 2: Train List. The extension searches trains, waits for booking to open, checks seats for your class and clicks Book Now as soon as it's available.
    • If IRCTC says We are experiencing High Load, the extension tries again by itself.
    • If IRCTC shows a message that won't go away by trying again, such as a wrong date or Unable to Process Request … Client IP, the extension stops and shows you the message. Continue by hand from there.
  • Step 3: Passenger Details. Passengers, mobile number and preferences are filled in and Continue is clicked for you.
    • IRCTC keeps loading for about 45 seconds after Book Now before moving on. This is normal for every user. Don't click Continue again.
  • Step 4: Review Journey. Most bookings have no captcha here now, and the extension clicks Continue.
    • If a captcha appears, a prompt shows the Current Seat Availability. Decide whether to go ahead, then enter the captcha and press Enter.
    • On some trains IRCTC asks for an Aadhaar OTP. Click Generate OTP, enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile, then click Continue. The extension waits for you.
  • Step 5: Payment. The extension picks your payment method and provider. Approve the payment in your UPI app quickly: seats are confirmed only after payment completes.

✅ The script will handle the rest automatically! 🚀

Avoid These While Booking 🚫

  • Don't refresh the page, use the browser's Back/Forward buttons, or open IRCTC in a new tab.
  • Don't double-click any button. While IRCTC shows its loading screen, wait.
  • Don't leave the IRCTC page idle for a long time.

These are the reasons IRCTC shows Sorry!!! Please Try again!! and logs you out.
